# Wrap builder photo shoot

Put the original photos in this folder. Any file names are fine. Don't send them through WhatsApp,
which shrinks them. These originals stay off the website: they get cut into pieces and only small,
optimized versions go on the site.

Shoot everything in one session, in the same spot, with freshly made food.

## Shot list

- [ ] **Bread**: one sheet of the bread you use for wraps, laid flat and fully in frame. Two photos.
- [ ] **Chicken**: a handful, freshly shaved, spread out so the pieces don't touch.
- [ ] **Beef**: the same.
- [ ] **Gyro**: the same.
- [ ] **Lettuce**: shredded the way you do it for wraps, spread out.
- [ ] **Tomatoes**: cut the way you do them for wraps, spread out.
- [ ] **Onions**: the same.
- [ ] **Cucumbers**: the same.
- [ ] **Pickles**: the same.
- [ ] **Turnips**: the same.
- [ ] **Finished wrap**: a grilled wrap in your branded paper, lying on the table, shot from straight
      above like your Facebook cover photo. Plus one cut in half, showing the inside.
- [ ] **Branded paper**: one sheet laid flat. The print file from your supplier is even better.

Nice to have:

- [ ] An open wrap with everything on it, just before it gets rolled.
- [ ] Each sauce drizzled across a plain plate: garlic, spicy mayo, tahini, hummus, tzatziki and hot sauce.

## How to shoot

- Straight down, from about the same height every time.
- A plain, matte background in a colour the food isn't: light grey for most things, a dark board for
  white things like garlic sauce and onions.
- Daylight or even kitchen light. No flash, filters or portrait mode.
- Leave about a finger's width between pieces.
